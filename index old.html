Buyer & Trade Policies

Clear terms for international B2B sourcing.

01

Supplier verification

Supplier identity, product grade, SDS/COA, export eligibility and commercial terms should be verified before any order is confirmed.

02

Direct shipping & customs

The supplier dispatches directly to the buyer. Freight terms, duties, taxes, import permits and destination-country compliance are stated in the written quotation. Required customs and shipping documents must show accurate legal details.

03

Commercial disputes

Cancellation, quality claims, delivery issues and commercial disputes follow the written quotation and the agreed supplier terms.

Privacy

Enquiry information is used only for sourcing, quotation, order coordination and customer support. Do not submit passwords, card numbers or other secrets through this page.

Product safety

The catalogue focuses on mainstream lower-hazard ingredients. Final suitability, regulatory status, SDS, labeling and transport classification must be confirmed for each transaction and destination.

Website security

This standalone front end does not collect passwords or confidential credentials. Keep API secrets on a secure server, not inside this HTML file.

Cosmic Bridge role

Cosmic Bridge manages sourcing, negotiation and buyer communication. Supplier identity can be kept off public catalogue pages, but must not be misrepresented and must be disclosed wherever law, customs, shipping or payment compliance requires it.
